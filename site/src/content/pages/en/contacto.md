Cartonería pieces are made by hand, one at a time. If you are interested in a work published on this site, would like to commission a piece with particular characteristics, or represent a gallery, a museum or a media outlet, tell us briefly what you have in mind. The workshop replies as soon as it can.

For commissions, it helps to indicate the type of figure (alebrije, skeleton, other), an approximate size and the dates by which you would like to receive it. Large pieces require weeks of drying and painting.
