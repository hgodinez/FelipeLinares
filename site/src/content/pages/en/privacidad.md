## What data is collected

This site uses no tracking cookies and no third-party analytics. The only personal data processed is what you choose to send through the contact form: your name, email address and the content of your message.

## What it is used for

Exclusively to reply to your inquiry. It is not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not added to mailing lists.

## Where it is stored

Messages are received in the workshop's email or through the form service configured by the site administrator. You may request deletion of your message by writing through the same channel.

## Sources and external links

The site links to external sources (museums, press, archives) to document the information published. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

## Controller

Felipe Linares Vargas, Mexico City. Direct contact details will be published once the workshop confirms them.
