    text: Alebrijes and skeletons modeled in paper and cardboard, hand-painted in the family workshop in Mexico City.
  - heading: The artist
    text: Felipe Linares Vargas belongs to the generation that received the craft from Felipe Linares Mendoza and, through him, from Pedro Linares López. He makes alebrijes, skeletons and seasonal figures in the tradition of Mexico City cartonería.
  - heading: A legacy of paper and cardboard
    text: In 1936, Pedro Linares López dreamed of creatures repeating an unknown word. Since then, five generations have given that dream shape with their hands.
  - heading: The workshop
    text: Paper, cardboard, paste, wire and brush. Each piece begins as an armature, is covered layer by layer, dries in the air and is painted with patience.
  - heading: Start a conversation
    text: Inquiries about works, commissions, collaborations with galleries and museums, and press. The workshop replies personally.
