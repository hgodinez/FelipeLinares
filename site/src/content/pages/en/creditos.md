## Works and photographs

All works reproduced on this site are by Felipe Linares Vargas and are protected by copyright. The photographs of works and of the workshop were provided for this site; individual photo credits will be added to each image once the workshop confirms them. No artwork photograph has been generated by artificial means.

Works documented without an image ("Tzompantli", 2010; rooster alebrije, 2026) will be shown with photographs only with permission from the institutions that hold or exhibit them.

## Documentary sources

The historical information on this site comes from verifiable public sources. Every artwork record, every profile and every archive entry links to its sources. The main ones are:

- Video testimonies of Felipe Linares Mendoza, Felipe Linares Vargas and Leonardo Linares Vargas (transcript provided by the family, 2026). The site's quotations come from there.
- Video stills (Felipe's portrait, Felipe painting, Felipe with his father at the table, pieces in the workshop and Pedro Linares López with the rooster Barbas): provided by the family; the video's title, producer and date are to be confirmed.
- Photograph of the three generations (Leonardo, to be confirmed, Felipe Linares Mendoza and Felipe Linares Vargas with a portrait of Pedro Linares López): provided by the family; author and date to be confirmed.
- Photograph of Felipe Linares Vargas and Felipe Linares Mendoza with life-size skeletons: provided by the family; place and date to be confirmed.
- Studio photograph of the goat alebrije with a rooster body (home page hero): provided by the family; photographer, date and title of the piece to be confirmed. The hero shows a digital cutout of that photograph (background removed, 2× enlargement); the piece itself is unaltered.
- Archival photograph of Pedro Linares López painting an alebrije: provided by the family; author, date and rights to be confirmed before definitive use.
- Museo Nacional de Culturas Populares (Ministry of Culture), object records and stories published on Google Arts & Culture.
- Ministry of Culture of the Government of Mexico, press release on the tribute to Pedro Linares (June 2025).
- Museum of International Folk Art, Santa Fe: exhibition "Alebrijes: A Linares Family Tradition" (2026–2028).
- Fowler Museum of Cultural History, UCLA: "En Calavera: The Papier-Mâché Art of the Linares Family", Susan N. Masuoka (1994).
- Encyclopedia.com: "Linares, Pedro (1906–1992)".
- Wikipedia: "Pedro Linares", "Linares family (alebrijes and cartonería)", "Alebrije" and "Desfile de alebrijes en Ciudad de México".
- Press: La Jornada (2026), Siempre! (2020), Excélsior (2019), CNN en Español (2024), National Geographic, KUNM and KRQE (2026).

## What has not been included

The quotations attributed to Felipe Linares Vargas and his family come from their video testimonies; no personal dates, awards or exhibitions have been published unless documented in the sources above. Pending items are marked on the site as "to be confirmed".
